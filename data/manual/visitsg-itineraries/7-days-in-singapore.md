## Day-wise shape (sample)

### Days 1–2 — Iconic city and waterfront
Marina Bay area highlights often include Gardens by the Bay, the bayfront promenade, and nearby museums. Evenings work well for skyline views. Mix outdoor boardwalks with indoor climate-controlled attractions when heat or rain peaks.

### Days 3–4 — Heritage neighbourhoods
Explore cultural precincts such as Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Gelam for temples, streetscapes, and local food. These districts are walkable hubs with MRT access and many covered walkways.

### Days 5–6 — Nature and family-friendly outdoor time
Sentosa and coastal parks suit outdoor play; choose indoor aquariums or science centres if weather turns. Wildlife and nature parks pair well with morning starts to avoid mid-day heat.

### Day 7 — Flexible wrap-up
Leave buffer time for shopping belts, a favourite hawker centre, or a missed museum. Revisit a neighbourhood café stretch or an indoor gallery if storms are forecast.

## Planning tips

- Alternate outdoor mornings with indoor afternoons.
- Cluster attractions by MRT line to reduce transit time.
- Family itineraries benefit from shorter hops and scheduled rest breaks.
- Keep one “rain swap” indoor option per day (museum, mall, cultural centre).

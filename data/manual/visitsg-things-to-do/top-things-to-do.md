## Must-see and skyline experiences

Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay waterfront, and Merlion Park anchor many first visits. Pair outdoor photo stops with nearby indoor galleries when humidity rises.

## Culture and heritage

Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Gelam offer temples, mosques, shophouses, and craft shopping. Cultural precincts are strong for half-day walks with frequent food stops.

## Food and local experiences

Hawker centres are central to Singapore food culture — try neighbourhood centres for noodles, rice dishes, and desserts. Food halls inside malls are useful indoor alternatives during heavy rain.

## Nature and outdoor time

East Coast Park, Southern Ridges walks, and island day trips suit outdoor energy. Start early, hydrate, and keep a covered café or museum as a weather pivot.

## Family-friendly picks

Science and discovery centres, aquariums, and gentle park trails work well with children. Build shorter segments and include air-conditioned breaks between outdoor blocks.

## Indoor vs outdoor quick guide

| Preference | Examples |
|------------|----------|
| Outdoor | Gardens by the Bay outdoor areas, waterfront promenades, park connectors |
| Indoor | Museums, aquariums, malls, cultural centres |
| Mixed | Heritage neighbourhoods with covered walkways and hawker centres |

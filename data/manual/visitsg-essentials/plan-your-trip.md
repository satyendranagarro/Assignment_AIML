## Climate and packing

Singapore is warm and humid year-round, typically about 25–32°C. Brief tropical showers are common; pack light breathable clothing, sunscreen, an umbrella, and indoor layers for strong air-conditioning. Outdoor sightseeing is comfortable in mornings and evenings; mid-day heat is intense.

## Language and connectivity

English is widely used alongside Malay, Mandarin, and Tamil. Visitor Wi‑Fi and prepaid SIM / eSIM options are easy to obtain at the airport and city retail points. Keep emergency numbers and hotel contacts offline as a backup.

## Getting around

The MRT and bus network covers major attractions. Contactless bank cards and stored-value cards work on public transport. Taxis and ride-hailing are plentiful. For multi-day stays, a tourist pass can simplify travel if you plan dense sightseeing days.

## Practical visitor tips

- Tap water is generally potable; carry a refillable bottle.
- Queue etiquette and orderly boarding are expected on public transport.
- Smoking is restricted in many public places; follow posted rules.
- Keep a digital copy of passport and travel insurance details.
- Hawker centres and malls are reliable indoor breaks during heavy rain.

## Services travellers use often

Airport arrival, tourist information counters, and major hotel concierges can point to transit cards, city maps, and attraction tickets. Plan indoor options (museums, malls, gardens under cover) for stormy afternoons so itineraries stay flexible.

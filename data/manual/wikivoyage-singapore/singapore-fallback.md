## Districts / neighbourhoods

Singapore’s visitor core includes the Colonial District / Civic District, Marina Bay, Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam, Orchard Road, and Sentosa. Neighbourhoods are compact and linked by MRT. Chinatown and Little India concentrate heritage temples and street food; Marina Bay concentrates modern skyline attractions.

## Get around

The Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) is the backbone for tourists. Buses fill gaps. Taxis and ride-hailing are widely available. EZ-Link / NETS FlashPay / contactless bank cards simplify fares. Walking between clustered sights downtown is practical in cooler hours.

## See (attractions)

Major sights include Gardens by the Bay, Merlion Park, the Singapore Botanic Gardens, Chinatown temples, and island attractions on Sentosa. Many outdoor icons have nearby indoor museums useful in tropical rain.

## Eat

Hawker centres (for example Maxwell Food Centre, Old Airport Road, Lau Pa Sat) are essential local food experiences. Expect rice, noodle, and grilled dishes at modest prices. Mall food courts are reliable air-conditioned backups.

## Stay safe and practical tips

Singapore is generally very safe. Tap water is drinkable. Dress modestly at religious sites. Carry an umbrella for short storms. English signage is common on transit and attractions.

## Three-day sketch

Day 1: Marina Bay + Gardens by the Bay (mix outdoor and indoor conservatories).  
Day 2: Chinatown and Little India walking loops with hawker meals.  
Day 3: Botanic Gardens or Sentosa; keep an indoor museum swap if weather turns.
